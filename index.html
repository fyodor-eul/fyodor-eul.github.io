About Me

I'm Fyodor. My real name is Thiha Htoo Zin. I started out studying Mechanical Engineering at Yangon Technological University, but I slowly realized what actually held my attention was wanting to understand computers themselves: the hardware, Linux, and the networks that tie it all together. That mattered enough that, after finishing three years of the program, I left the Bachelor's degree behind in my fourth year to pursue a Diploma in Information Technology at Temasek Polytechnic instead, where I'm now a Year 2 student.

Since then, I've cared most about how systems work underneath: the kernel, the network stack, the parts that quietly decide whether everything else works. I'd rather rebuild something from raw primitives than just use it, which is why side projects tend to replace things I could have just installed: Unresolved is a DNS resolver I wrote in C instead of just using one, and MinimalContainerRuntime is a container engine built straight on Linux namespaces instead of reaching for Docker.

That same habit carries into how I build backend systems. When I built SecureMemberPortal, I treated each feature as an exercise in closing a specific class of vulnerability rather than just shipping working code. I'm trying to grow from someone who can make things work into someone who can make things hold up.

I run Arch Linux with Hyprland and Vim, an environment shaped by hand, not accepted by default, and I write about what I learn along the way so the notes are still useful the second time I read them.
